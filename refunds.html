Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Operating Business: MD Jedan Hossain

1. Nature of Digital Products

The Construction Estimation Master Toolkit™ consists of digital Excel/spreadsheet templates delivered electronically upon successful payment. Because digital products are instantly accessible and non-returnable once downloaded, sales are generally considered final. However, we strictly prioritize customer satisfaction and handle genuine grievance requests under our fair refund policy below.

2. Refund Eligibility & Conditions

You may be eligible for a full refund under the following specific circumstances within 7 days of purchase:

  • File Defect / Corruption: The downloaded template file is corrupted, defective, or unopenable, and our technical support team is unable to provide a working replacement file within 48 hours.
  • Non-Delivery of Download Link: Payment was successfully debited from your account via Cashfree Payment Gateway, but you did not receive access/download links, and support could not resolve it.
  • Accidental Duplicate Purchase: You accidentally purchased the exact same toolkit twice in a single session (in which case 100% of the duplicate transaction will be refunded).

⚠️ Note: Refund requests based on change of mind or lack of compatible software (e.g. not having Excel/Google Sheets installed) after successful file download are not eligible for refund. Please ensure spreadsheet compatibility prior to purchasing.

3. Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled prior to digital file delivery/download. Once payment is completed and instant download access is granted or emailed to you, order cancellation is not permitted due to the instant nature of digital media.

4. How to Request a Refund (Step-by-Step)

If you meet the refund criteria above, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an Email: Write an email to xtechmax2024@gmail.com with the Subject Line: Refund Request - [Your Order ID].
  2. Provide Transaction Details: Include your full name, registered email address, transaction reference / Cashfree Payment ID, and a brief description of the technical issue.
  3. Review & Verification: Our support team will review your request within 24 to 48 business hours. We may reach out to assist with technical troubleshooting or verify duplicate charges.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your refund request is approved:

⏱️ Processing Time: 5 to 7 business days

Approved refunds are processed directly back to your original payment method (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or Wallet) through Cashfree Payment Gateway.

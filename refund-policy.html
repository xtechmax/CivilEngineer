1. Product Type

All products sold on this website are digital products (PDF e-books and downloadable files). Once a purchase is made and the product is delivered, the transaction is generally considered final.

2. Refund Eligibility

We offer refunds in the following limited circumstances:

  • Non-delivery: If you did not receive the product within 24 hours of successful payment and our team is unable to re-deliver it

3. Refund Not Applicable

Refunds will NOT be issued in the following cases:

  • The digital product has been successfully downloaded and/or delivered to your email

Since these are digital products that are delivered instantly upon successful payment, cancellations are not possible once the payment has been processed and the product delivered.

If you wish to cancel before payment is completed, simply close the checkout window — no charge will be applied.

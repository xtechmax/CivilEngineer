Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Firm: M/S Solution Legal Advisory

Service: Professional Legal Consultation & Strategy Session (₹2,000)

Support & Disputes: consult@xtechmax.shop | +91 89108 03089

1. Consultation Fee Structure

The consultation fee of ₹2,000 covers reserved professional time with our Senior Advocate (such as Adv. Sahin, Adv. Srilekha, Adv. Rukshana, or Adv. Danish), case document assessment, and confidential legal advice protected under Attorney-Client Privilege.

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Rescheduling: Clients may reschedule their consultation session at any time up to 2 hours before the scheduled time slot without any additional fee.
  • Cancellation Before Advocate Assignment: If a client requests cancellation before our legal desk has assigned the advocate or initiated document review, a 100% refund will be processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment source.
  • After Consultation Delivered: Once the 1-on-1 consultation session with the advocate has been conducted, the fee is deemed fully rendered and non-refundable.

3. Technical Failures or Gateway Errors

In the event of duplicate payment debits, network drops, or gateway errors, excess deductions are automatically identified and refunded back to your bank account within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Fee Adjustment Policy

If you decide to engage M/S Solution for full formal litigation representation (such as filing High Court petitions, SARFAESI stay petitions, or formal OTS bank settlement representation), the ₹2,000 consultation fee is credited and adjusted against the total retainership fee.

5. Dispute Resolution Contact

For any refund queries, please email us at consult@xtechmax.shop or send a WhatsApp message to +91 89108 03089 with your booking reference.

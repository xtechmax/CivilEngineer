Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our legal consultations, advocate sessions, and confidentiality.

Can recovery agents legally visit my home or call my family members?
No, absolutely not. Under RBI's Master Directions on Fair Practices Code, recovery agents are strictly prohibited from contacting your relatives, visiting your home without prior notice, making abusive calls, or contacting you before 8:00 AM or after 7:00 PM. Any such action amounts to criminal intimidation and violation of privacy. Our advocates issue formal legal notices that immediately stop this harassment.
How does a One-Time Settlement (OTS) work, and how much can I save?
In an OTS, we legally represent you before the bank's NPA settlement committee. Based on your genuine financial distress, banks legally waive penal interest, accumulated charges, and a major portion of the principal. In unsecured loans and credit cards, we routinely secure 50% to 75% total waivers, followed by a formal No Dues Certificate ensuring you are permanently released from liability.
Why has the cyber police frozen my bank account, and how do you unfreeze it?
If someone deposits money that originated from a cyber scam (even unwittingly through P2P, freelancing, or crypto), the cyber cell places a debit freeze on all accounts downstream in that transaction trail. We file a Section 457 CrPC petition before the concerned Magistrate Court, establish the legitimacy of your funds, and secure an official judicial order directing the bank to unfreeze your account.

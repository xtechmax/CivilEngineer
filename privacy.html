1. Attorney-Client Privilege

At M/S Solution, all consultations, case briefings, loan details, cyber crime evidence, and communications are strictly protected under Attorney-Client Privilege. Under Indian law (Section 126 & Section 129 of the Indian Evidence Act), an advocate cannot disclose any professional communication made by a client without the client's express written consent.

2. Information We Collect

When you book a legal consultation or contact our intake desk, we collect only the necessary details required to formulate legal strategy:

  • Your Name, Email Address, Phone/WhatsApp Number, and City.
  • Case details: Bank name, loan type, notice copies, recovery agent details, or cyber scam transaction logs.
  • Payment confirmation data processed securely through RBI-licensed payment aggregators (PayU). We never store your full card number or banking credentials.

3. Non-Disclosure Guarantee

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, marketing agencies, or financial institutions. Your data is used exclusively by our designated advocates (Adv. Sahin, Adv. Srilekha, Adv. Rukshana, Adv. Danish, and case managers) to provide you with legal assistance.

4. Data Storage and Security

All digital case files, notices, and payment references are stored with 256-bit encryption. Access is restricted to certified legal personnel bound by strict non-disclosure agreements.

5. Contact Data Privacy Officer

If you have any questions regarding how your case details are handled, contact our privacy desk at consult@xtechmax.shop.
